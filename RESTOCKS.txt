WHEN THE WILLIAMSPORT-AREA STORES RESTOCK POKEMON - AND HOW PEOPLE FIND OUT
Researched 2026-09-21 in two passes (six research runs). The short version of each store's line
is in its "notes" on the site.


BOTTOM LINE

No store here has a publicly known restock day. The second pass tried vendor job postings,
per-store pages on restock trackers, Reddit, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, X, local news, and
Yelp/Google reviews. None of it names a day for any of these stores. So the site's "usual
day" field (restockDays) is deliberately left empty everywhere: filling it with guesses would
show them as fact.

What is known:
  - Who stocks each store.
  - Roughly how often.
  - The exact question to ask to get the real day.
Once a store has a real day, put it in restockDays (or tell me) and the site shows the next
date. Logging visits works too: after three restocks the site works out the day on its own.


BY STORE

Target Williamsport (Muncy, #2437) - who: known; day: unknown
  Evidence
    - Stocked by Excell Marketing. Excell's merchandiser job for this route lists the store's
      own address: posted 2026-06-05, full time, $17-18/hr, still open on 9/21. The route may
      be short-staffed, which would mean skipped or irregular weeks.
    - Excell's postings: each store is visited about weekly, Mon-Fri, sometimes Saturday,
      between 6am and 6pm, several stores a day, with the rep setting the route.
    - The same Excell route also covers Wilkes-Barre and Dickson City.
  What that means: one vendor visit a week on a weekday, most likely a morning. The day can
  move from week to week.
  Trackers disagree about Target in general (Sun/Mon early, Tue-Thu, Friday), so ignore them.
  ASK: Guest Services or electronics, "Which weekday does the Excell card vendor usually come?"
  Also: limits are company-wide (1-2 per item); Target.com drops mostly land 3-6am ET.

Walmart Montoursville (#1887), Lewisburg (#1644), Mill Hall (#2528) - who: unconfirmed; day: unknown
  Evidence
    - Walmart cards are stocked by MJ Holding or Excell reps. Which one serves these three
      stores isn't public.
    - Excell's Muncy route runs weekdays in daytime and visits each store about weekly.
      Montoursville is 8-10 miles away and Lewisburg is inside that route's 75-mile radius;
      Mill Hall is probably on another route.
    - MJ routes can be run by partner merchandisers and can skip weeks (a 2021 report from
      Oklahoma).
    - TCG Drop Radar claims Walmart shelves get worked Wed/Thu 7-10am. It cites nothing.
      One blog claims an MJ window of Thursday to Saturday. It cites nothing either.
  ASK: the electronics or toy department, "What day does the trading-card vendor come?"
  Or look at the company name on the stocker's boxes (Excell or MJ Holding); that tells you
  whose schedule you're learning.
  Also: limit 5 card items per visit. Walmart.com waves often come Wed 9pm-midnight ET, and
  there's an online-only lottery ("Collectibles Drawing") for some drops. Each store has a
  Facebook page (Walmart1887, Walmart1644, Walmart2528); none posts about Pokemon.

GameStop Kriner Plaza (Montoursville, #7796) - day: release days (medium confidence)
  - New sets go out on release day. Some GameStops hold midnight launches: they did for Chaos
    Rising (May 22) and 30th Celebration (Sep 16, "no pre-order required, while supplies
    last"). Whether #7796 takes part is unknown; ask.
  - Next release: Delta Reign, Fri Nov 6.
  - Preorders are back, in store only and priced above list: the Delta Reign ETB is $90
    against a $49.99 list, and preorders opened Aug 20.
  - Between releases, refills are small, unannounced and on no known day. "Tuesdays" or
    "Tue-Thu" claims online are unsourced.
  - Limit 1 per customer, 2 for Pro ($25/yr). Pro gets early access to some preorders.
  - The store hosts Pokemon TCG play on alternate weekends (r/Williamsport, Aug 2026). Staff
    are reachable there. Phone (272) 202-2980.

The White Knight's Game Room (637 Rose St, Williamsport) and "In Haven" (110 E Main St, Lock
Haven) - day: release days (low confidence, industry norm)
  - Both are official Play! Pokemon venues in Pokemon's event locator.
  - Lock Haven "stocks Pokemon cards and accessories" (Lock Haven Express, Mar 2026).
  - When stock arrives isn't posted anywhere public. Hobby stores normally put new sets out on
    release day. Stores running a prerelease may sell from the Monday before, and Lock Haven
    ran a midnight prerelease for Chaos Rising. Delta Reign prerelease events (Oct 24-Nov 1)
    aren't listed anywhere in the US yet.
  - League nights, the easiest time to ask about Delta Reign preorders and allocation:
      Williamsport: Tue, Wed and Thu 5-9pm; Sat open play
      Lock Haven:   Thu 5:30-8pm; the locator also lists Saturdays
  - Williamsport has a separate "White Knight's Game Room Pokemon" Facebook page for events;
    it's the likeliest place for product posts.
  ASK: "Are you taking Delta Reign preorders, and will you have ETBs or booster boxes on
  release day?"

Pokemon Center (online) - day: Tue/Wed most likely (medium confidence)
  - Day of week, from 39 US releases since Jan 2025 (Restockd): Wed 33%, Tue 31%, Mon 23%,
    Thu 10%, Fri 3%. Weekends never.
  - Time of day: late morning to early afternoon ET. TCG Drop Radar's 2026 log puts 7 of 10
    queues at 12:08-1:13pm; Restockd says 45% around 11am.
  - A queue opens first and product goes live about 13 minutes later.
  - Delta Reign preorders weren't open as of Sep 7. The 2026 lead times (37-63 days) point to
    about Sep 28-Oct 2; that's a projection, not an announcement.
  - Newsletter subscribers can get invite-only Early Access emails (since Apr 2026).
  - Alerts: @PokemonRestocks and @PokeTCGAlerts on X, Discord monitors, restock apps.
  - Limits are per product, often 2 (the 30th Celebration ETB). Bot-driven preorders were
    mass-cancelled on Sep 7-8, 2026, and some legitimate buyers were caught too.


ONLINE DROPS AT THE BIG CHAINS (restock trackers' logs, not official)
  - Target.com: 92% of drops landed 3-6am ET, usually capped at 2 per order.
  - Walmart.com: bigger waves often Wednesday 9pm-midnight ET; Walmart+ members sometimes get
    early access; the online lottery launched Aug 31, 2026.
  - GameStop.com: some preorders go early to Pro members.


HOW RESELLERS GET THE INFORMATION
  - Paid Discord "cook groups" ($8-10 a month). They run monitors on retailer sites and pool
    members' in-store sightings. Some allow checkout bots.
  - Local Facebook groups and TikTok. Nearest general groups: "Williamsport pa" (33K) and
    "Williamsport Area Buy Sell And Trade" (28K). No Pokemon-specific local group was found.
  - Asking staff and vendor reps. This often fails, because reps set their own routes.
  - Stock checkers (Target's per-store availability, BrickSeek). They lag behind the shelf.
  - Learning the vendor day by showing up repeatedly, which is what logging here does for you.
  - Camping out for launches.
  - Over the line: following vendor reps from store to store, putting AirTags on their cars,
    running bots, or using multiple accounts to get past limits. Stores and police push back
    on these, and tracking someone's car is stalking.


A ROUTINE THAT FITS THIS SITE
  1. Ask the one question listed for each store, and send me the answers. They go into
     restockDays and the site starts showing next dates.
  2. On any visit, log what you see, including "Out". That's what turns into restock dates
     when nobody will tell you the day.
  3. When staff give a specific date, put it in "Next restock expected" when you log.
  4. Online: Pokemon Center late mornings on Tue/Wed, Target.com early mornings, Walmart.com
     Wednesday nights.
