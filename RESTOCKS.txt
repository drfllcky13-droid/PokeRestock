WHEN THE WILLIAMSPORT-AREA STORES RESTOCK POKEMON - AND HOW PEOPLE FIND OUT
Researched 2026-09-21. The short version of each store's line is in its "notes" on the site.


BOTTOM LINE

No store here publishes its Pokemon restock days, and no one online has posted them for these
stores either: nothing turned up on Reddit, Facebook, TikTok or restock sites. The reason is
structural:
  - At Target and Walmart the cards belong to a vendor, not the store. The vendor's reps (Excell
    Brands, MJ Holding) stock them on their own routes, and the day differs from store to store.
    Store staff often don't know it.
  - GameStop ships Pokemon straight to its stores without announcing it.
  - The local game stores get whatever their distributor allocates.

So the only way to learn a particular store's day is to watch it. Every "Out" and "Restocked"
you log counts. After three restocks the site shows "usually <weekday>" and a next date.


BY STORE

Target Williamsport (Muncy, #2437)
  - Vendor-stocked. Each Target gets its own weekday vendor visit, usually around opening time.
  - Purchase limits are now set company-wide; for big launches it's 1 per item.
  - For the 30th Celebration launch, employee reports described extra security, no saving line
    spots with chairs, and lines from 10:30pm the night before.
  - Target posts nothing about restocks. Its "Target Store Williamsport" Facebook page only shares
    corporate posts.

Walmart Montoursville (#1887), Lewisburg (#1644), Mill Hall (#2528)
  - Vendor-stocked by MJ Holding and Excell reps.
  - One unsourced blog says the MJ window is Thursday through Saturday with times varied on
    purpose. Treat that as unconfirmed.
  - Limit: 5 trading-card items per visit, enforced at the register since Nov 2025.
  - Each store has its own Facebook page, run by store staff: facebook.com/Walmart1887, /Walmart1644
    and /Walmart2528. None has posted about Pokemon. Mill Hall posted on Sept 3 that flooding had
    closed its front entrance.

GameStop Kriner Plaza (Montoursville, #7796)
  - Pokemon ships straight to stores; drops follow set releases and aren't announced.
  - In May 2025 one chain-wide restock showed up only as notices in store windows.
  - Limit: 1 per customer, 2 for GameStop Pro members ($25 a year).
  - No store social media. Phone (272) 202-2980.
  - The Lewisburg GameStop is closed; it was removed from the list.

The White Knight's Game Room (637 Rose St, Williamsport) and "In Haven" (110 E Main St, Lock Haven)
  - They buy on allocation from hobby distributors, and allocations of hot sets get cut hard: for
    Prismatic Evolutions the average store got 10-15% of what it ordered.
  - Stores that run prereleases may sell a new set from the Monday before its US release date.
    Lock Haven ran a midnight prerelease for Chaos Rising in May 2026.
  - Pokemon nights, where you can just ask what's coming in:
      Williamsport: Tue, Wed and Thu 5-9pm; Sat open play
      Lock Haven:   Thu 5:30-8pm
  - Where they post:
      Williamsport: Facebook TheWhiteKnightsGameRoomEriksEdibles, Instagram @thewhiteknightsgameroom
      Lock Haven:   its Facebook page, which does post Pokemon events

Pokemon Center (online)
  - Weekdays only. Every drop logged in 2026 opened between 10am and 1:15pm ET, most often in the
    11am hour.
  - A queue opens first and product goes live about 13 minutes later. Joining the queue seconds
    earlier makes a big difference.
  - No schedule is published.


ONLINE DROPS AT THE BIG CHAINS (from restock trackers' logs; not official)
  - Target.com: 92% of drops landed 3-6am ET, usually capped at 2 per order.
  - Walmart.com: bigger waves often Wednesday 9pm-midnight ET, and Walmart+ members sometimes get
    early access. There is also an online "Collectibles Drawing" lottery, which offered its first
    Pokemon item on Aug 31, 2026.
  - GameStop: no online preorders from Feb 2025. A tracker account says they returned in Jan 2026;
    unconfirmed.


HOW RESELLERS GET THE INFORMATION
  - Paid Discord "cook groups" ($8-10 a month). They run monitors on retailer sites and pool
    members' in-store sightings. Some allow checkout bots.
  - Local Facebook groups and TikTok, where people post "restocked at X" and ask "what day does
    your Walmart restock". Nearest general groups: "Williamsport pa" (33K members) and
    "Williamsport Area Buy Sell And Trade" (28K). No Pokemon-specific local group was found.
  - Asking staff and vendor reps. This often fails, because reps run their own routes.
  - Stock checkers such as Target's per-store availability and BrickSeek. They lag behind the shelf.
  - Being there on the right morning: learning a store's vendor day by showing up repeatedly,
    which is what logging here does for you.
  - Camping out for launches.
  - Over the line: following vendor reps from store to store, putting AirTags on their cars,
    running bots, or using multiple accounts to get past per-order limits. Stores and police push
    back on these, and tracking someone's car is stalking.


A ROUTINE THAT FITS THIS SITE
  1. On any visit, log what you see, including "Out". That is what turns into restock dates.
  2. Ask at a White Knight Pokemon night what's arriving and when; local shops will usually say.
  3. When staff at a Target or Walmart give you a date, put it in the report's "Next restock
     expected" field. The site shows it until the date passes.
  4. For online drops, keep weekday late mornings in mind (Pokemon Center), Target.com early
     mornings and Walmart.com Wednesday nights, or use a free tracker's alerts.
